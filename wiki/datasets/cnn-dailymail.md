# CNN/Daily Mail

Also written CNN/DM and CNNDM in the sources.[^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024][^yanDecodingSpeculativeDecoding2025]

## Overview

CNN/Daily Mail is a text summarization dataset.[^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024] All four papers that use it in the wiki are [speculative decoding](../methods/speculative-decoding.md) papers, and they use it as the summarization task for measuring decoding speedup.[^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024][^liEAGLE3ScalingInference2025][^weiSDFPSpeculativeDecoding2026][^yanDecodingSpeculativeDecoding2025]

## Composition

- It comprises abstractive summary bullets generated by humans from news stories on CNN and Daily Mail websites, presented in the form of questions with entities hidden.[^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024]
- Sizes and splits are not stated in the wiki's papers.

### Protocols

- Zhou et al. report ROUGE scores on the validation split, primarily ROUGE-2, and use a maximum input length of 2,048 and a maximum output length of 128 for distillation and evaluation.[^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024] For latency they sample 500 examples from the test or validation set and measure wall-clock decoding time at batch size 1.[^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024]
- Li et al. follow EAGLE and [Spec-Bench](../benchmarks/spec-bench.md) and use the same weights for all tasks without fine-tuning on the respective tasks.[^liEAGLE3ScalingInference2025]
- Wei et al. use a maximum output length of 64, a 1-shot protocol, 1,000 randomly sampled test instances, batch size 1, and greedy decoding.[^weiSDFPSpeculativeDecoding2026]
- Yan et al. pick the setting where self-speculative decoding achieves its highest speedup: LLaMa-2-70B as target model with greedy sampling on CNNDM and [XSum](xsum.md).[^yanDecodingSpeculativeDecoding2025]

### Task quality

No ROUGE value for CNN/Daily Mail is given in the text or tables of the wiki's papers.

- Zhou et al. report ROUGE-2 on CNN/DM only in figures.[^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024]
- Li et al. do not evaluate generation quality, because EAGLE-3 does not modify the target model's weights and uses strict speculative sampling acceptance conditions.[^liEAGLE3ScalingInference2025]
- Wei et al. do not evaluate generation quality either, on the ground that speculative decoding theoretically guarantees an unchanged output distribution.[^weiSDFPSpeculativeDecoding2026]

## Known issues

- The speed tables are not comparable across papers. Target models, drafting methods, output lengths, and shot counts differ, and each paper measures speedup against its own baseline.[^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024][^liEAGLE3ScalingInference2025][^weiSDFPSpeculativeDecoding2026][^yanDecodingSpeculativeDecoding2025]
- Yan et al. print speedup as a percentage, while the other three papers print a ratio.[^yanDecodingSpeculativeDecoding2025][^liEAGLE3ScalingInference2025][^weiSDFPSpeculativeDecoding2026][^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024]
