# C4

## Overview

The wiki's papers use C4 in two roles:

- **Prompt source for speed tests.** SpecExec and SpecEdge draw prompts from it to measure [speculative decoding](../methods/speculative-decoding.md) speed. SpecEdge names the English subset, C4 (en).[^svirschevskiSpecExecMassivelyParallel2024][^parkSpecEdgeScalableEdgeAssisted2025]
- **Pre-training domain.** C4 is one of the seven domains of [RedPajama](redpajama.md), which Sheared-LLaMA uses for pruning and continued pre-training.[^xiaShearedLLaMAAccelerating2024]

No paper in the wiki reports a task-quality metric (accuracy, perplexity, ROUGE) on C4.

## Composition

Sizes and splits are not stated in the wiki's papers. Within RedPajama, C4 accounts for $15.0\%$ of the original data proportion.[^xiaShearedLLaMAAccelerating2024]

### Protocols

- Svirschevski et al. use subsamples of size 100 from each of their prompt datasets and measure the speed of generating 32+ tokens per prompt.[^svirschevskiSpecExecMassivelyParallel2024] They run the non-chat model tests on C4.[^svirschevskiSpecExecMassivelyParallel2024]
- Park et al. generate up to 256 output tokens per query, report metrics over the output tokens after prefill, and use off-the-shelf target and draft models without fine-tuning.[^parkSpecEdgeScalableEdgeAssisted2025]

### C4 as a RedPajama domain

- With dynamic batch loading, the share of C4 in the data that Sheared-LLaMA loads rises from $15.0\%$ (original RedPajama proportion) to $49.2\%$. The paper reads this as the Book and C4 subsets being more challenging for a pruned model to recover.[^xiaShearedLLaMAAccelerating2024]
- Estimated reference loss on the C4 domain: 2.125 for LLaMA2-1.3B and 2.033 for LLaMA2-2.7B.[^xiaShearedLLaMAAccelerating2024]

## Known issues

- The same pair, Sheared Llama-1.3B as draft and Vicuna-33B as target, appears in both speed papers with different numbers, under different settings. SpecExec reports 33.3 tok/s and a 2.26x speedup at t=0.6 with a draft budget of 128, in-memory on an A100.[^svirschevskiSpecExecMassivelyParallel2024] The server-only baseline of SpecEdge reports a server throughput of 28.12 tok/s and a 1.86x speedup at temperature 0.7 with draft tree size 32, on an A100 server.[^parkSpecEdgeScalableEdgeAssisted2025] The settings differ, so these rows are not a contradiction.
