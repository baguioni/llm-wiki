# Nemotron Post-Training Dataset

## Overview

DFlash calls it the NVIDIA Nemotron Post-Training Dataset V2 and cites it as Nathawani et al. 2025, with a link to the Hugging Face dataset `nvidia/Nemotron-Post-Training-Dataset-v2`.[^chenDFlashBlockDiffusion2026] Osprey cites the same dataset, Nemotron-Post-Training-Dataset-v2, as Nathawani et al. 2025 together with NVIDIA 2025, the Nemotron Nano 2 model report.[^bieOspreyTargetagnosticPretraining2026]

Three papers in this wiki use it to train [speculative decoding](../methods/speculative-decoding.md) drafters or a drafter's starting weights. DFlash builds its main training mixture from it,[^chenDFlashBlockDiffusion2026] PARD-2 adds samples from "Nemotron-v2" and "Nemotron-v3" to the PARD data,[^anPARD2TargetAlignedParallel2026] and Osprey trains the diffusion warm-start model for its DFlash ablation on it.[^bieOspreyTargetagnosticPretraining2026] None of them evaluates on it.

No paper in the wiki states the size, splits, or format of the dataset itself.

## Usage across papers

| Paper | Use | Setting | Numbers |
|---|---|---|---|
| [DFlash](../papers/chenDFlashBlockDiffusion2026.md) | Main training data for the DFlash draft models: a mixture of around 800K samples from Nemotron Post-Training Dataset V2 and CodeAlpaca, with responses generated by the target model for better target alignment | Qwen3 4B, 8B, and Coder-30B-A3B-Instruct targets; 6 epochs, AdamW, learning rate $6\times 10^{-4}$, maximum sequence length 3072 tokens (4096 for Qwen3-Coder) | On Qwen3 models with the Transformers backend, an average speedup of 4.9$\times$ over autoregressive decoding under greedy decoding, a 2.4$\times$ improvement over EAGLE-3 (16)[^chenDFlashBlockDiffusion2026] |
| [PARD-2](../papers/anPARD2TargetAlignedParallel2026.md) | Samples from "Nemotron-v2" and "Nemotron-v3" added to the PARD training set, which keeps Magpie and Evol-CodeAlpaca | Llama-3.1-8B, Qwen3-8B, and Qwen3-14B drafters; 4, 2, and 2 training epochs | On Qwen3-8B (target-dependent mode), average speedup 5.81$\times$ and average acceptance length 6.98; on Llama3.1-8B, 5.19$\times$. The number of Nemotron samples is not stated[^anPARD2TargetAlignedParallel2026] |
| [Osprey](../papers/bieOspreyTargetagnosticPretraining2026.md) | Training data for a compact Introspective Diffusion Language Model (IDLM) built on a four-layer LayerSkip-Llama backbone; its transformer blocks initialize a DFlash drafter body (Appendix G) | Qwen3-8B target; each DFlash configuration then fine-tuned for six epochs on a 65k single-domain math split, block size 16 | Under the original DFlash-style interface, the IDLM warm start gives math (in-domain) accepted length 6.03 against 5.96 for a random body, and an OOD average of 1.68 against 1.64. The EAGLE-style DFlash variant with the IDLM warm start reaches an OOD average of 2.00[^bieOspreyTargetagnosticPretraining2026] |

## Known issues

- PARD-2 does not use the dataset's full name. It writes "Nemotron-v2 [3]" and "Nemotron-v3 [4]", and its references [3] and [4] are model reports, "Nvidia nemotron nano 2: an accurate and efficient hybrid mamba-transformer reasoning model" (arXiv:2508.14444) and "Nemotron 3 nano: open, efficient mixture-of-experts hybrid mamba-transformer model for agentic reasoning" (arXiv:2512.20848).[^anPARD2TargetAlignedParallel2026] Osprey cites the Nemotron Nano 2 report next to Nemotron-Post-Training-Dataset-v2.[^bieOspreyTargetagnosticPretraining2026] No paper in the wiki names a "Nemotron-Post-Training-Dataset-v3", so the PARD-2 row on this page rests on the paper's short names, not on an explicit dataset citation.
- DFlash mixes the dataset with CodeAlpaca and regenerates the responses with the target model, and it does not give the share of each source in the around 800K samples.[^chenDFlashBlockDiffusion2026] Its LLaMA-3.1-8B-Instruct drafter is trained on [UltraChat](ultrachat.md) and [ShareGPT](sharegpt.md) instead, to match EAGLE-3's training data.[^chenDFlashBlockDiffusion2026]
- Osprey does not give the number of samples, steps, or tokens used to train the IDLM warm-start model.[^bieOspreyTargetagnosticPretraining2026]
