# Drafter training data across papers

## Question

What datasets do the drafter papers in this wiki train on, how large are they in samples, and are the answers regenerated by the target? This sizes the data budget for building a drafter for a new target, see [How can a mobile-efficient drafter be built cheaply for a new target?](../questions/cost-efficient-drafter-construction.md)

## Answer

Full drafter recipes train on about 0.5M to 1.4M prompts whose answers are regenerated by the target. Adaptation and ablation runs work with 65K to 100K samples. Epochs range from 1 to 10 and maximum sequence length from 512 to 8K, so the sample count alone does not measure training cost. Open-PerfectBlend is the most common prompt source: DSpark, SpecForge, DPara, and Osprey's Llama-3.3-70B run all use it.

### Size statements that differ between papers

- ShareGPT is approximately 68K entries in EAGLE-3[^liEAGLE3ScalingInference2025] and 120K conversations in SpecForge.[^liSpecForgeFlexibleEfficient2026] UltraChat is approximately 464K entries in EAGLE-3[^liEAGLE3ScalingInference2025] and 200K conversations in SpecForge.[^liSpecForgeFlexibleEfficient2026]
- Open-PerfectBlend is 1.3 million samples in DSpark[^chengDSparkConfidenceScheduledSpeculative2026] and 1.4M conversations in SpecForge.[^liSpecForgeFlexibleEfficient2026] These disagreements are tracked in [How large are the standard drafter training sets?](../questions/drafter-training-set-sizes.md)

### Data composition and regeneration matter

- SpecForge's EAGLE-3 reproduction for LLaMA-3.1-Instruct reaches an acceptance length of 2.82 on Math500 when trained on ShareGPT and UltraChat, and 3.48 when trained on PerfectBlend.[^liSpecForgeFlexibleEfficient2026]
- In the same paper, regenerating the answers raises acceptance length on nearly all benchmarks (FinanceQA is the exception) and average throughput by 5.3%.[^liSpecForgeFlexibleEfficient2026]
- EAGLE-3 reports that more training data raises its speedup, a scaling curve it says earlier EAGLE versions did not show.[^liEAGLE3ScalingInference2025]
- PARD's Qwen drafter trains on regenerated answers only, and its Llama3 drafter on regenerated plus original answers.[^anPARDAcceleratingLLM2025]

## Caveats

- "Samples" means different things: prompts (DSpark, LK Losses, Osprey), entries (EAGLE-3), or conversations (SpecForge). Multi-turn conversations hold more tokens than single prompts.
- Several papers name a dataset without saying how many of its samples they used (PARD, PARD-2, DPara, HAT).
- Training cost depends on samples × sequence length × epochs, and for feature drafters also on the target forward passes. See [What the target must supply to train a drafter](target-signals-for-drafter-training.md).
