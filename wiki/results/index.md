# Results

* [Discarding context at interceptions costs 37-40% of model forwarding time](recomputation-share-of-forwarding-time.md) - InferCept measures that recomputing discarded context takes 37-40% of total model forwarding time on a mixed augmented-LLM workload.
* [Speculative decoding speedup falls from 2.73× to 1.31× as batch size grows from 1 to 32](speculative-decoding-speedup-vs-batch-size.md) - Su et al. measure that the speedup of speculative decoding shrinks as batch size grows and that the best speculation length gets shorter.
* [Test-time scaling with a verifier raises SWE-bench Verified scores above single-attempt scores](swe-bench-verified-test-time-scaling.md) - R2E-Gym and SWE-RM both report large gains on SWE-bench Verified from selecting among many rollouts with a verifier.
* [WebRL lifts open models to 42.4-49.1 average success rate on WebArena-Lite](webrl-webarena-lite.md) - WebRL trains Llama3.1 and GLM-4 web agents to 42.4-49.1 average success rate on WebArena-Lite, above GPT-4-Turbo at 17.6.
* [Block drafters reach 1.6–1.8× the throughput of autoregressive drafters on a phone NPU](block-drafters-vs-ar-drafters-on-phone-npu.md) - In my phone-drafts, server-verifies evaluation for Qwen3-14B, the one-decode block drafters (PARD-1, PARD-2, DFlash, DSpark) reach 26.7-30.0 tok/s with their best trees against 17.6-18.5 for the per-level autoregressive drafters (Qwen3-0.6B, EAGLE-3).
* [Drafting on the server GPU instead of the phone NPU](server-vs-phone-draft-location.md) - Drafting on the server GPU instead of the phone NPU raises throughput for every drafter tested; as chain7 in PyTorch bf16 the six drafters gain ×1.22-1.36 and PARD-2, DSpark, and DFlash (31.0-34.0 tok/s) beat the best phone arm, while the earlier Qwen3-0.6B-only runs gave ×1.09 as a chain and ×1.30 in tree16.
* [HyCo cuts server GPU time per token by 38.6% against server-side MTP on Spec-Bench](hyco-server-gpu-time-vs-cloud-mtp.md) - Drafting on a Galaxy S26 Ultra NPU with the verifier's own MTP head plus one SLM pass, HyCo needs 38.6% less server GPU time per output token than server-side MTP self-speculation on Spec-Bench (27.7% with exact verification) while delivering 35.0 tokens/s.
* [A pruned-target PARD-1 drafter reaches 74% of a same-recipe Qwen3-0.6B drafter's MAT](pruned-target-pard-drafter-vs-small-model.md) - Minitron-pruning Qwen3-14B to the Qwen3-0.6B shape, chat-KD recovery on 57M tokens, and PARD-1 training give offline MAT 1.96 at K=8 on Spec-Bench, against 2.66 for Qwen3-0.6B with the same data and recipe and 3.07 for the released amd/PARD-Qwen3-0.6B; the gap follows the backbone's first-token accuracy (0.570 against 0.751).
* [I-DLM-8B outpaces EAGLE-3 in per-request throughput on Qwen3-8B](i-dlm-vs-eagle-3-throughput.md) - In SGLang on one H100, I-DLM-8B (a Qwen3-8B converted to an introspective diffusion LM) generates 341 tok/s per request on MATH-500 at concurrency 1 against 238 for Qwen3-8B with an EAGLE-3 chain draft, and stays ahead up to concurrency 32; the bit-for-bit lossless variant reaches 310 tok/s.
