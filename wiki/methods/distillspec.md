# DistillSpec

## Overview

DistillSpec uses white-box [knowledge distillation](../methods/knowledge-distillation.md) (KD) to align the draft model with the target model before applying [speculative decoding](../methods/speculative-decoding.md) (SD).[^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024] Its goal is a higher acceptance rate of drafted tokens, not a better task score for the student.[^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024] It makes two design choices: on-policy data generated by the draft model, and a divergence function tailored to the task and decoding strategy.[^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024]

## How it works

- **Objective.** The draft model $\mathscr{M}_q^\theta$ is trained to minimize a divergence $D$ between the target and draft next-token distributions over a training set $\mathscr{G}$. White-box access to both models is assumed, so both next-token distributions and samples from both models are available.[^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024]
- **Why a divergence matters.** The token-level acceptance rate equals $1 - D_{\operatorname{TVD}}(p(y_t), q(y_t))$, so maximizing the sequence-level acceptance rate is equivalent to minimizing the expected TVD between target and draft.[^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024]
- **Choice of divergence.** Directly optimizing TVD does not give the best performance in most of the settings explored. The divergence (FKL, RKL, JSD, TVD) is treated as a hyperparameter to tune for the task and decoding strategy.[^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024]
- **Choice of training data.** The paper proposes generating the distillation data with the draft model, because it gives similar or superior performance compared to target-generated data at a much lower cost.[^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024]
- **Theorem 4.1.** The paper bounds the acceptance rate from below in terms of the on-policy KD loss.[^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024]
- **Training.** Adafactor optimizer, 300,000 training steps, batch size 32, learning rate 0.0003, and KD-loss temperatures of both models held at 1.0.[^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024]

## Variants

KD algorithms compared as the distillation step (Table 1 of the paper):[^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024]

| Name | Divergence | Training Data ($\mathscr{G}$) |
|---|---|---|
| SeqKD (black-box KD) | FKL | Data generated by teacher $\mathscr{M}_p$ |
| Supervised KD | FKL | Fixed dataset of input-output pairs |
| ImitKD | FKL | Fixed dataset + data generated by $\mathscr{M}_q^\theta$ |
| f-Distill | TVD | Data generated by $\mathscr{M}_q^\theta$ and $\mathscr{M}_p$ |
| On-policy GKD | FKL / JSD | On-policy data from student $\mathscr{M}_q^\theta$ |

- **Best recipe per setting (block efficiency).** XSum with greedy decoding: data from both models, with JSD slightly ahead. GSM8K with greedy decoding: FKL with only draft-generated data. GSM8K with temperature sampling ($T=1$): RKL with target-generated data.[^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024]
- **Model garden.** With several model sizes available, the paper's recommended strategy is to first distill a large model into a smaller one that serves as the target, then apply DistillSpec to distill an even smaller draft.[^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024]
- **Lossy SD.** The paper also combines the distilled draft with lossy speculative decoding, using lenience functions to control the quality-latency trade-off.[^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024]

## Papers using it

- [DistillSpec](../papers/zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024.md) proposes the method. Setup: T5-XL (3B) target with T5-Small (77M) draft on [XSum](../datasets/xsum.md), [CNN/DM](../datasets/cnn-dailymail.md), WMT, and [GSM8K](../datasets/gsm8k.md), and a GPT-like 234M target with a 33M draft on LM1B. Latency is wall-clock time at batch size 1 on a TPUv4 device.[^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024]
  - Speedup over standard SD: 10$-$45% in the abstract, the contribution list, and the Figure 1 caption; Section 5.1 states 10$-$46%.[^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024]
  - Transfer: a draft distilled on GSM8K, evaluated on 23 BigBenchHard tasks, raises the average speedup from 1.93$\times$ and 1.78$\times$ to 2.21$\times$ and 2.02$\times$ for greedy and non-greedy decoding. The Figure 1 caption gives an average speedup of $26\%$.[^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024]
  - Model garden: relative latency falls from 17.3 to 2.7 on XSum (6.4$\times$) and from 15.0 to 1.4 on GSM8K (10.7$\times$). Rouge2 on XSum goes from 23.1 to 23.0, and accuracy on GSM8K from 33.1 to 34.8.[^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024]
  - Supervised KD on ground-truth data ranks lowest in all settings. f-Distill and GKD, which use only model-generated data, outperform the other variants. SeqKD performs poorly although it trains on target-generated data, which the paper takes as evidence that white-box supervision from the target's logits is vital for SD.[^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024]
- [LK Losses](../papers/samarinLKLossesDirect2026.md) cites DistillSpec as prior work that goes beyond the standard KD framework. It describes DistillSpec as exploring reverse KL and total variation (TV) distance as KD objectives for already pretrained LMs used as external speculators, and as concluding that the choice of divergence depends highly on the task and data.[^samarinLKLossesDirect2026] It contrasts its own setting: native draft modules tightly integrated into the target model and trained from scratch, not separately pretrained external drafters.[^samarinLKLossesDirect2026]

## Strengths & Limitations

- Distilled drafts transfer to unseen tasks (the BigBenchHard result above).[^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024]
- No single recipe is best: the divergence and the data construction must be tuned per task and decoding strategy.[^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024]
- TVD is the theoretically motivated objective, yet its direct optimization does not give the best performance in most settings explored.[^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024] LK Losses notes that in DistillSpec the draft is initialized from a pretrained language model, while in its own setting the draft is initialized randomly, and states that this distinction is critical.[^samarinLKLossesDirect2026]
- Improvements in SD and in downstream task performance are only weakly correlated: a high-quality distilled model is not necessarily an effective draft model.[^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024]
- The method assumes white-box access to both models.[^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024]
- Gains on WMT are small: +0.05× to +0.12× in Table C.1.[^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024]
- Latency is measured at batch size 1.[^zhouDistillSpecImprovingSpeculative2024]
