# Osprey

## Overview

Osprey builds an [EAGLE-3](eagle-3.md)-style autoregressive drafter whose body starts from a pretrained small language model instead of random weights. The expensive pretraining is done once and needs no target model; each target then gets the same per-target adaptation budget as standard EAGLE-3 training.[^bieOspreyTargetagnosticPretraining2026]

## How it works

1. **Prune.** Keep the embedding, LM head, final norm, and the first $N$ blocks of a small LM. The main backbone is Qwen3-4B with 2 of its blocks kept, at hidden size 2560.[^bieOspreyTargetagnosticPretraining2026]
2. **Pretrain (target-agnostic, once).** Next-token prediction on FineWeb-100BT for $55$k steps (approximately $462$B tokens), sequence length $4096$.[^bieOspreyTargetagnosticPretraining2026]
3. **Convert (per target).** Resize the embedding and LM head to the target vocabulary, and add the EAGLE-3 fused target features through zero-initialized QKV columns, so that the drafter starts out computing its pretrained function.[^bieOspreyTargetagnosticPretraining2026]
4. **Adapt (per target).** Distill the frozen target with EAGLE-3's [training-time test](training-time-test.md), with loss $\sum_{k}\lambda^{k}\operatorname{CE}$ and $\lambda=0.8$.[^bieOspreyTargetagnosticPretraining2026]

The implementation is built in SpecForge on top of [SGLang](sglang.md).[^bieOspreyTargetagnosticPretraining2026]

The paper labels Stage 2 "off-policy" pretraining and says Stage 4's TTT loss "can be viewed as an on-policy multi-step distillation against the target".[^bieOspreyTargetagnosticPretraining2026] The Stage 4 sequences are still prompt–response pairs regenerated by the target, for example about $65$k prompts per Qwen3-8B domain.[^bieOspreyTargetagnosticPretraining2026] Within the TTT unroll the input tokens are teacher-forced, and what comes from the drafter is its own hidden state, which becomes part of its context at the next step.[^bieOspreyTargetagnosticPretraining2026] Stage 4 is therefore not on-policy in [DistillSpec](distillspec.md)'s sense of whole sequences sampled from the draft.

## Variants

- **[LayerSkip](layerskip.md) backbone.** A 4-layer LayerSkip-Llama backbone at hidden size 2048 (294 M body parameters) is also evaluated.[^bieOspreyTargetagnosticPretraining2026]
- **Warm start for a block drafter (Appendix G).** A diffusion LM ([IDLM](i-dlm.md)) on a four-layer LayerSkip-Llama backbone is used to warm-start a [DFlash](dflash.md) drafter for Qwen3-8B.[^bieOspreyTargetagnosticPretraining2026]

## Papers using it

### Osprey paper ([Bie et al. 2026](../papers/bieOspreyTargetagnosticPretraining2026.md))

SGLang, batch size 1, 5 steps, top-$k$ 1, 6 draft tokens, temperature 0. AL is the mean accepted length.[^bieOspreyTargetagnosticPretraining2026]

| Target | Baseline | Baseline mean AL | Osprey mean AL | Throughput (tokens/s) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen3-8B (5×5 cross-domain grid) | 1-layer EAGLE-3 from scratch | 3.176 | 3.686 | not given for the grid |
| Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct | EAGLE-3 reproduced / official | 2.45 / 2.33 | 2.97 | 155.8 / 150.3 vs 183.7 |
| MiniMax-M2.5 | public EAGLE-3 checkpoint | 2.634 | 3.232 | 187.1 vs 219.8 |

All rows: [^bieOspreyTargetagnosticPretraining2026]

- At equal depth, a 2-layer EAGLE-3 trained from scratch gains $0.094$ AL over the 1-layer version, and Osprey beats the 2-layer EAGLE-3 by $0.405$ AL. The authors attribute the gain to the pretrained language prior rather than capacity.[^bieOspreyTargetagnosticPretraining2026]
- Body parameters on Qwen3-8B: Osprey 265 M at width 2560, 1-layer EAGLE-3 269 M and 2-layer EAGLE-3 487 M at width 4096.[^bieOspreyTargetagnosticPretraining2026]
- On Qwen3-8B, Osprey's throughput gain over EAGLE-3 is 24% at batch size 1 and 13% at batch size 16.[^bieOspreyTargetagnosticPretraining2026]

## Strengths & Limitations

- The per-target cost equals EAGLE-3's (48 H100-hours for Qwen3-8B), and pretraining needs no target forward pass.[^bieOspreyTargetagnosticPretraining2026]
- Pretraining is an upfront cost of 3,040 H100-hours for $55$k steps. On MiniMax-M2.5 the paper estimates it is recovered after approximately $3.5$ billion generated tokens.[^bieOspreyTargetagnosticPretraining2026]
- Osprey is less sensitive to the adaptation learning rate than a from-scratch EAGLE-3.[^bieOspreyTargetagnosticPretraining2026]
- Under DFlash's [KV injection](kv-injection.md) interface, the warm start gains at most $0.07$ accepted tokens in any domain. The authors attribute this to key/value distribution drift.[^bieOspreyTargetagnosticPretraining2026]
- Most results come from single runs.[^bieOspreyTargetagnosticPretraining2026]
- **Which stages matter (Qwen3-8B; chat, code, finance, math; overall AL).** A 2-layer EAGLE-3 from scratch reaches 3.385, the pruned Qwen3-4B body without Stage 2 reaches 3.605, and the full pipeline reaches 3.790.[^bieOspreyTargetagnosticPretraining2026] That puts about $+0.22$ on the inherited pruned weights and $+0.185$ on Stage 2 (my arithmetic across Tables 5 and 6; the pruned body is narrower, 2560 against 4096). Stage 2's gain is front-loaded: the 10k-step checkpoint is within $0.026$ AL of the 55k-step one.[^bieOspreyTargetagnosticPretraining2026] The paper does not ablate Stage 3's zero-initialized QKV expansion, its vocabulary alignment, the kept depth $N$, or the Stage 4 loss weight $\lambda$.
