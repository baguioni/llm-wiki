# Experiments

* [On-device draft cost on the Galaxy S25 Hexagon NPU](npu-draft-cost-profiling-galaxy-s25.md) - My profiling of draft-model decode latency, memory, and energy on a Samsung Galaxy S25 NPU across 60 layer-by-width shapes, with the live DFlash depth sweep.
* [Six phone drafters for a Qwen3-14B server verifier](drafter-evaluation-qwen3-14b.md) - My live evaluation of six Q4_0 drafters (PARD-1, PARD-2, DFlash, DSpark, Qwen3-0.6B, EAGLE-3) drafting on a Galaxy S25 NPU for a Qwen3-14B-FP8 verifier on a DGX Spark, in chain, matched-tree, and own-best tree shapes; block drafters reach 26.7-30.0 tok/s against 13.0-18.5 for the autoregressive ones, and every block-drafter arm beats server-only speculative decoding with a GPU Qwen3-0.6B draft (17.0-21.6 tok/s), though the block drafters themselves run faster as server-only chains (28.0-34.0 tok/s).
* [Pruning Qwen3-14B into a PARD-1 drafter](minitron-pard-qwen3-14b.md) - My pipeline that one-shot Minitron-prunes Qwen3-14B to the Qwen3-0.6B shape (0.75B with untied embeddings), recovers it by chat-KD on 57M tokens of the target's own answers, and PARD-1-trains it; offline on Spec-Bench it reaches MAT 1.96 at K=8 against 2.66 for a Qwen3-0.6B control with the same data and recipe and 3.07 for the released amd/PARD-Qwen3-0.6B.
* [Six drafters on the server GPU, chain7, no phone](server-only-chain-drafter-evaluation.md) - My server-only counterpart of the phone chain7 arms, with all six drafters re-implemented in PyTorch bf16 and drafting on the same DGX Spark GB10 as the Qwen3-14B-FP8 target; every drafter is 1.22-1.36× faster than on the phone, PARD-2 (34.0 tok/s) and DSpark (33.9) tie for first, and three chain arms beat the best phone arm (30.0).
