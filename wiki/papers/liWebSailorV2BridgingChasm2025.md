# WebSailor-V2: Bridging the Chasm to Proprietary Agents via Synthetic Data and Scalable Reinforcement Learning

Text source: arXiv HTML, version `2509.13305v1`.

## Summary

The paper attributes the gap between open-source web agents and proprietary deep research systems to two problems: training data with insufficient diversity and a narrow definition of uncertainty (mostly obfuscation), and the lack of a scalable reinforcement learning (RL) training environment.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]

[WebSailor-V2](../methods/websailor-v2.md) is a complete post-training pipeline that covers data construction, Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT), and RL.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025] The data side is [SailorFog-QA-V2](../datasets/sailorfog-qa-v2.md), built from a densely interconnected knowledge graph with a wider variety of uncertainties. The training side is a dual-environment RL framework: a simulator for low-cost algorithm iteration and a managed real-world environment for final policy training, connected to data generation by a feedback loop.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]

Trained on Qwen3-30B-A3B, WebSailor-V2 scores 35.3 on BrowseComp-EN, 44.1 on BrowseComp-ZH, and 30.6 on Humanity's Last Exam (HLE).[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]

## Contributions

- SailorFog-QA-V2, an enhanced dataset built upon [SailorFog-QA](../datasets/sailorfog-qa.md), with improved knowledge graph construction and sampling and a wider variety of uncertainty definitions beyond obfuscation.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]
- A simulated RL environment based on a large-scale offline Wikipedia knowledge base, for high-frequency algorithmic experimentation and data curation.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]
- A systematically managed toolkit for RL training in the real environment, addressing consistency of tool returns, reproducibility of trajectory sampling, high concurrency, and fault tolerance.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]
- A feedback loop between data construction and RL training that synthesizes and filters data based on training dynamics.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]

## Method

### Agentic framework

- The agent uses the [ReAct](../methods/react.md) framework. A trajectory with $T$ iterations is $\mathcal{H}_{T}=(\tau_{0},a_{0},o_{0},\dots,\tau_{T},a_{T})$, where $\tau_{i}$, $a_{i}$, $o_{i}$ are thought, action, and observation.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]
- The authors choose ReAct deliberately for its simplicity, to isolate the intrinsic capabilities of the model from prompt engineering or framework design.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]
- The action space has four tools plus the terminal action *final answer*.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]

| Tool | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Search | Google search; accepts multiple queries and returns the top-10 results for each, with title, snippet, and URL |
| Visit | Takes web pages with a goal for each; Jina retrieves the full content and a summary model (Qwen3-30B-A3B) extracts information relevant to the goal |
| Google Scholar | Search within the academic domain |
| Python interpreter | Sandboxed execution of code generated by the agent |

All rows: [^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]

### SailorFog-QA-V2

- **Graph construction.** As in SailorFog-QA, construction starts from a seed entity and uses web tools to discover related entities. V2 deliberately establishes denser connections between nodes and creates cyclic structures, because iterative "easy-to-hard" expansion tends to produce tree-like or acyclic structures. V2 also keeps procedural information (search queries, source URLs) and stores statistical features per entity.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]
- **Subgraph extraction.** Exhaustive enumeration of substructures is infeasible on the denser graph, so V2 uses a random-walk based approach and collects non-isomorphic connected subgraphs, verified by the Weisfeiler-Leman algorithm.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]
- **QA generation.** The pipeline first analyzes how many non-isomorphic nodes exist in a topology so that the QA focus is spread evenly across all orbit nodes. It introduces a wider array of defined uncertainties beyond obfuscation.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025] The paper does not list the uncertainty types or the dataset size.

### SFT cold start

- The SFT dataset is composed entirely of synthetic data generated from SailorFog-QA-V2. Trajectories are produced by open-source models solving the generated QA tasks, with rejection sampling.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]
- The base model is Qwen3-30B-A3B-Thinking-2507, with the context length increased to 128k.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]
- SFT hyper-parameters (Megatron): batch size 64, learning rate 5e-6 with a minimum of 1e-10, warmup plus cosine decay schedule, weight decay 0.1.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]

### Agentic reinforcement learning

- **Simulated environment.** Built from an offline Wikipedia database and a matching suite of web tools. The SailorFog-QA-V2 pipeline is adapted to this offline corpus to create training and testing data for the simulation.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]
- **Real environment.** A unified tool execution interface with a scheduling and management layer. Each tool has QPS constraints, result caching, automated timeout-and-retry protocols, service degradation for non-critical failures, and switching to backup data sources.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]
- **Data curation.** An automated data synthesis and filtering pipeline adjusts the training set in real time, guided by training dynamics.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]
- **RL algorithm.** A tailored adaptation of [GRPO](../methods/grpo.md) with a clipped objective using $\varepsilon_{low}$ and $\varepsilon_{high}$, and advantage $\hat{A}_{i,t}=R_{i}-\text{mean}(\{R_{i}\}_{i=1}^{G})$. Training is strictly on-policy, uses a token-level policy gradient loss, and uses a leave-one-out strategy for advantage estimation.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]
- **Negative samples.** An unfiltered set of negative trajectories degrades training stability and can lead to "format collapse" after extended training. Some negative samples are excluded from the loss, for example those that give no final answer because they exceed a length limit.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]
- Dynamic sampling is not used; larger batch and group sizes are used instead.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025] No explicit entropy regularization or bonus is used.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]
- RL hyper-parameters (rLLM): temperature 1.0, $top_{p}=1.0$, batch size 128, learning rate 1e-6.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]

## Experiments & Results

### Setup

- Model: SFT and RL training on Qwen3-30B-A3B-2507.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]
- Training data: primarily SailorFog-QA and SailorFog-QA-V2, supplemented with IterBench for mathematical and academic reasoning.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]
- Benchmarks: [BrowseComp-EN](../benchmarks/browsecomp.md), [BrowseComp-ZH](../benchmarks/browsecomp-zh.md), [GAIA](../benchmarks/gaia.md) (a subset of 103 cases from the text-only validation subset), [xbench-DeepSearch](../benchmarks/xbench-deepsearch.md), [Humanity's Last Exam (HLE)](../benchmarks/hle.md), and [DeepResearch Bench](../benchmarks/deepresearch-bench.md).[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]
- Metric: pass@1 with temperature 0.85 and top-p 0.95, with an LLM as judge for accuracy.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]
- Inference: context length 128k and a maximum of 100 ReAct iterations.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]

- On xbench-DeepSearch and GAIA, the paper states that the agent leads the open-source field and surpasses the strongest proprietary systems.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]
- On HLE the paper claims a new state-of-the-art at 30.6, above DeepSeek-V3.1 (29.8) and OpenAI-o3 (20.2).[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]
- The SFT-only model already scores 24.4 on BrowseComp-EN and 23.9 on HLE. The paper calls the SFT cold start a prerequisite for RL, because rewards are sparse and an agent without a competent initial policy rarely completes tasks.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]

### DeepResearch Bench (Figure 3)

WebSailor-V2 scores 48.9 on the overall score defined in DeepResearch Bench, second to Gemini-2.5-pro-DeepResearch at 49.7.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025] The authors attribute the gap to their training focus on information retrieval and synthesis, with less emphasis on the stylistic quality of the final report.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]

### Analyses

- **Training dynamics (Figures 4 and 6).** Training reward rises with training steps, and BrowseComp-EN and BrowseComp-ZH validation performance rises with oscillation. On BrowseComp, pass@1 and pass@3 rise together. On xbench-DeepSearch and GAIA, pass@1 improves significantly while pass@3 gains are marginal, which the paper reads as RL improving sampling efficiency on tasks already within the model's base capabilities.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]
- **Entropy (Figure 7).** Policy entropy stays at a consistently high level and oscillates without a clear trend. The paper hypothesizes that this follows from the non-stationary web environment.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]
- **Context scaling (Figure 5, BrowseComp-EN).** Accuracy rises with available context length before converging. Nearly 90% of the correctly solved instances are completed within a context of 64k. At a 32k context limit, WebSailor-V2 achieves an accuracy of around 16 on BrowseComp-EN. WebSailor-V1 used a 32k context and a 72B dense model.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]
- **Training on the test set.** Training the model directly on the BrowseComp testing set gave substantially poorer results than training on the synthetic data. The authors hypothesize that the synthetic data offers a more consistent distribution, whereas the human-annotated data is noisier and limited in scale. No numbers are given.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]
- **Algorithm versus data.** The authors state that data and the stability of the training environment are likely more critical than the specific RL algorithm in determining whether agentic RL works.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]
- **Case study.** A BrowseComp case is solved after a reasoning process spanning 29 steps.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]

## Limitations

- Proprietary agents were not tested across all benchmarks, because not all are fully accessible via API; several are evaluated manually through their websites, and some cells are missing due to cost constraints.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]
- GAIA is evaluated on a subset of 103 cases from the text-only validation subset only.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]
- On DeepResearch Bench the model trails Gemini-2.5-pro-DeepResearch; the pipeline places less emphasis on the quality of final report generation.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]
- The paper notes that for truly difficult problems even pass@3 may not reflect the upper bound of the model's capabilities.[^liWebSailorV2BridgingChasm2025]
- The paper reports no ablation numbers for the data curation loop, the simulated environment, the uncertainty types, or the negative-sample filtering, and does not give the size of SailorFog-QA-V2 or of the SFT set.
- The paper has no dedicated limitations section.
